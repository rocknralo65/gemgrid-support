GemGrid: Rhinestone Pictures

Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

GemGrid is free and has no accounts. Your pictures stay on your device and are never sent to us. The app shows a video ad only when you choose to watch one, and those ads come from Google, which collects some information as described below.

What stays on your device

  • Your pictures. Photos you take or choose, the stone pictures made from them, your progress and your finished pictures are stored only in the app on your device. We never see them, and we have no server that receives them.
  • Camera and Photos. The app uses the camera or opens your photos only when you tap the button to add a picture, and only reads the picture you choose. Saving a finished picture to Photos adds that one image. You can change these permissions any time in Settings.
  • Printing goes straight from your device to your printer using Apple's printing system.

Ads

GemGrid is paid for by optional video ads. An ad plays only when you tap the wand button and choose to watch a video, in return for filling in a color. Nothing else in the app shows ads.

The ads are provided by Google AdMob. To load ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • your device's internet address and general location (such as country or city);
  • information about your device and the app, such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, such as whether a video was watched or tapped;
  • your device's advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking when iOS asks.

If you allow tracking, Google may use this information to show ads based on your interests and may combine it with information from other apps and websites. If you don't, you still get ads, just not personalized ones. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before Google uses your information for personalized ads.

Google handles this information under its own policies: how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We don't receive any of it in a form that identifies you.

Picture search

When you search for pictures, your search words are sent to Openverse, a free library of openly licensed images, and the pictures you see are loaded from the websites that host them (such as Wikimedia Commons and Flickr). Those services receive your search and your device's internet address, as any website does. The ten pictures built into the app need no internet at all.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ins, names or email addresses.
  • No analytics of our own, and we never sell or rent information.
  • We never upload your photos or pictures.

Children

GemGrid is a relaxing picture game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Don't want ads? Simply don't tap the wand. The rest of the app works without ever watching one.
  • Turn tracking on or off any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • You can turn off camera and Photos access in Settings.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's update notes.
